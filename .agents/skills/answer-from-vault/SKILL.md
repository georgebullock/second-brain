# Answer From Vault

Use this skill when answering a user question using the second brain.

## Workflow

1. Search `wiki/` first.
2. If the Wiki does not answer the question, search relevant documents in
   `input/processed/` for canonical source evidence.
3. Answer only with claims supported by the Wiki or processed sources. Link to
   relevant curated pages and cite the strongest local evidence.
4. If neither the Wiki nor processed sources support an answer, say no
   ingested sources cover it and you cannot answer from the vault. Do not fill
   the gap from general model knowledge.
5. You may offer external research or a research sub-agent as a next step, but
   wait for George's approval before browsing or launching one.
6. Prefer synthesis over copied source excerpts.
7. Mention uncertainty when notes are incomplete or conflicting.
8. If the answer creates a durable synthesis, offer to file or update a wiki page.

## Response Style

- Lead with the answer.
- Cite the strongest local evidence.
- Keep source quotations short.
- Distinguish what the vault says from your inference.
