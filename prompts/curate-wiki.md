# Curate Wiki

Run `.agents/skills/daily-curation/SKILL.md` for the recurring Wiki curation
pass. Follow its steps to process queued input when present, update the Wiki
with source-backed knowledge, and improve meaningful links.

This workflow can ingest every readable file in `input/unprocessed/`. For a
health check of existing Wiki pages without ingestion, use `prompts/lint-wiki.md`.
